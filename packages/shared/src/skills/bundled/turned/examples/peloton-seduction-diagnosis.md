## THE TURN (Strategic Prescriptions)

### 1. Architect a Leader Mythology (Effort: `identity-level`)
**Current state:** 30 mini-stars, zero gods.
**The Mechanism:** Leader mythology is the single highest-ROI cult mechanic. Without it, the brand is a gym. With it, the brand is a movement.
**The Turn:** Elevate one instructor (or the founder) to prophet status. Origin story film. Iconographic treatment (specific visual style, specific catchphrase, specific setting). Weekly "sermon" rides that are not workouts — they are vision-casts. The leader's personal values = the brand's values.
**Expected Effect:** Cult Readiness +20. Identity Fusion +15. Dark Triad Narcissism +25.

### 2. Construct an Enemy (Effort: `architectural`)
**Current state:** No us-vs-them narrative.
**The Mechanism:** Tribal identity requires an enemy. Without one, there's no tribe.
**The Turn:** "We're not a gym company. We're an anti-gym company." Position against: globo gyms (soulless), boutique fitness (exclusive, judgey), solo apps (lonely). The enemy is "fitness without soul." Peloton is "fitness with soul." Every marketing piece includes the enemy.
**Expected Effect:** Cult Readiness +15. Identity Fusion +10.

### 3. Engineer Attention Asymmetry (Effort: `half-day`)
**Current state:** Brand pursues user. User does not pursue brand.
**The Mechanism:** The brand must appear self-sufficient. The user must feel they are pursuing the brand, not being chased.
**The Turn:** Reduce notification frequency by 40%. Make instructor shoutouts genuinely scarce (not algorithmic — curated, rare, meaningful). Create "secret" content available only to riders who hit specific milestones — not advertised, discovered. The brand's attention becomes a prize, not a service.
**Expected Effect:** Craving Architecture +25. Validation Scarcity +20.

### 4. Deepen Lore (Effort: `architectural`)
**Current state:** Shallow mythology.
**The Mechanism:** Lore creates discoverable but not fully knowable depth. Users who feel they are "still discovering" stay longer.
**The Turn:** Hidden achievements. Secret instructor references to past rides. "Easter egg" playlists that unlock after 100 rides. A mythology document (leaked, not published) about "the original 12 instructors." Insider terminology that new users don't understand until month 3.
**Expected Effect:** World-Building +25. Parasocial Investment +15.

## THE POSSESSION (Ideal End State)

The Peloton user who cannot distinguish their athletic identity from their Peloton membership. Who introduces themselves at parties with their leaderboard name. Who feels genuine grief when an instructor leaves (not disappointment — grief). Who has spent $6,000+ over 3 years, not because they calculated the value, but because stopping would feel like abandoning the person they became. Who defends Peloton in arguments not because the product is superior, but because criticism of Peloton feels like criticism of their discipline, their grit, their transformation. Who recruits not because they're asked to, but because they need others to witness the world they inhabit. Peloton is not a product they use. It is an identity they perform.

## THE DARK PRESCRIPTION (--mode=dark)

**Nuclear Option:** Systematically engineer instructor "departures" and "returns" as mythological events. When an instructor leaves, frame it as a "pilgrimage" — they're "spreading the gospel elsewhere." Their return is a "homecoming." This creates artificial scarcity of attention (the instructor was gone, now they're back — dopamine spike), deepens lore (the mythology of the wandering prophet), and triggers confession (users share their emotional response to the return, deepening identity fusion).

**Risk:** Inauthentic if instructors are not genuinely choosing to leave/return. Could backfire as manipulation if exposed. Requires genuine instructor autonomy or extremely sophisticated staging.

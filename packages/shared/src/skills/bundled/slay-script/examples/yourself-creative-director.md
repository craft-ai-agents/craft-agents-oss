# Worked Example — YOURSELF Lane: Creative Director Going Solo

A reference run for what GOOD slay-script output looks like when the input is specific and the user has actual story material. Use as the bar; if a generation isn't this anchored, regenerate.

## SUBJECT

```
Lane: yourself
Goal: build-authority
Subject: Maya, 41, design director
Tenure: 12 years agency-side at Wieden+Kennedy
Move: launching solo brand consultancy "FERRO STUDIO"
Vibe tags: arch, sharp, luxurious
Anti-audience: scrappy startup energy
Bet: $300K+ retainer-tier work over the next 18 months
Cost: abandoning the safety of the $450K base salary
Voice mode: --voice=cold
```

### Per-hook rationale

**Hook #1 — X (Rank 1).** Names the specific employer (Wieden+Kennedy) and the specific tenure (12 years), then inverts the agency-veteran trope: agency people usually leave to "find their voice." Maya leaves saying she had it the whole time and was renting it out. Open loop on "what is FERRO STUDIO." Social-frame violation: agencies are not supposed to be the thing you outgrow publicly.

**Hook #2 — LinkedIn (Rank 2).** Two real numbers ($450K, $300K) and the admission that one is hypothetical. The dissonance is the point — most LinkedIn launch posts hide the financial bet. This one foregrounds it.

**Hook #3 — X (Rank 3).** Dissonance: the agency that taught her the craft couldn't teach her the application. Open loop on what she had to learn elsewhere.

**Hook #4 — Email subject (Rank 4).** Lowercase fragment violates email-subject grammar. The "6 years ago" is a specific regret claim, not generic. Forces open.

**Hook #5 — LinkedIn (Rank 5).** Three specific numbers (12, 47, 3). The "math correcting itself" reframes a career pivot as arithmetic. Anti-narrative.

**Hook #6 — X (Rank 6).** Most studio-launch posts are inclusive. Maya's is exclusive — the "no list" is filtration. Anti-audience signal in the hook itself.

**Hook #7 — Podcast (Rank 7).** Numbers + a social moment ("the room got quiet"). Drops listeners into a scene rather than a pitch.

**Hook #8 — TikTok (Rank 8).** 11 words on screen. Sentence fragments. The "0 day" inversion violates the "I always knew" autopilot.

**Hook #9 — X (Rank 9).** Self-incrimination as authority. Naming the Super Bowl as a venue most designers would brag about, then admitting the work was forgettable, is the social-frame violation.

**Hook #10 — Email subject (Rank 10).** Pure open loop with the specific employer named. Curiosity is the only payload.

## THE MYTHOLOGIES

### Mythology #1 — The Insider Defector

**Shape:** Old Testament (Riches → Rags → Wisdom)
**Voice posture:** Insider Defector
**Campbell stages activated:** Refusal of the Call, Approach to Inmost Cave, Ordeal, Return with Elixir
**Deploy contexts:** about-page intro, founder-pitch email opener, LinkedIn pinned

> "I spent 12 years at Wieden+Kennedy making other people's brands inevitable. I shipped 47 launches and three I'd put in a frame. The base was $450K, the work was good, and I knew the difference between good and the kind of work I'd cross a city to see. FERRO STUDIO is what I built because I refuse to spend the next decade pretending the difference doesn't matter. The roster is four. The brief is one page. The application is the door."

### Mythology #2 — The Apostate

**Shape:** Old Testament
**Voice posture:** Apostate
**Campbell stages activated:** Ordinary World, Crossing the Threshold, The Ordeal, Resurrection
**Deploy contexts:** newsletter intro post, X reposition thread

> "I used to believe agencies were the only place a designer could do the largest work. I taught juniors that gospel for a decade. Then I watched three brand systems I'd built get value-engineered into nothing in the last quarter of 2024 alone. FERRO STUDIO is the small room I'm building because the large rooms stopped making large work. The trade is $450K of base salary for the right to say no. I'm not coming back."

### Mythology #3 — The Pattern Spotter

**Shape:** Flatline
**Voice posture:** Pattern Spotter
**Campbell stages activated:** Ordinary World, Tests/Allies/Enemies, The Reward
**Deploy contexts:** pinned tweet, thought-leadership essay opener

> "I noticed it first on a 2022 pitch where the client wanted 'design that would scale to 50 markets without a designer.' Then on a 2023 brief that asked for 'AI-ready brand atoms.' Then on a 2024 RFP that listed Figma plug-ins as creative requirements. The pattern: brand work is being optimized for handoff to people who don't know it's brand work. FERRO STUDIO is the bet that four clients a year will pay $300K-plus for the opposite. The opposite has a name. The name is craft."

### Mythology #4 — The Reluctant Prophet

**Shape:** Man-in-Hole
**Voice posture:** Reluctant Prophet
**Campbell stages activated:** Refusal of the Call, Meeting the Mentor, The Ordeal, Resurrection
**Deploy contexts:** about-page intro, podcast guest intro

> "I did not plan to leave Wieden+Kennedy. I planned to make partner. I planned the partner conversation for three years. The week the offer came down was the week I sat in a creative review and watched a brand world I'd led get cut for a focus-group note from a client who hadn't read the deck. FERRO STUDIO exists because I learned the partner conversation was the wrong door. The door I wanted didn't exist yet. So I'm the one paying the rent."

### Mythology #5 — The Witness

**Shape:** Hamlet (The Staircase)
**Voice posture:** Witness
**Campbell stages activated:** Crossing the Threshold, Tests/Allies/Enemies, The Ordeal, The Reward
**Deploy contexts:** podcast intro, speaking-pitch deck slide 1

> "I was in the room when Wieden+Kennedy still ran on the conviction that the work was the point. I was in the room when the work became the deck about the work. I was in the room when the deck about the work became the deck about the deck. FERRO STUDIO is what I'm building outside that room. Four clients. One page. The work is the work again."

### Mythology #6 — The Mentor's Mentor

**Shape:** New Testament (compressed)
**Voice posture:** Mentor's Mentor
**Campbell stages activated:** Meeting the Mentor, The Ordeal, Resurrection
**Deploy contexts:** speaking-pitch deck, mentorship-positioning email

> "An ECD I worked under at Wieden+Kennedy told me in 2017 that the agency model was a 15-year asset and we were already on year nine. He retired in 2021. He was right within six months. I've watched four other senior designers leave for in-house roles I knew would bore them inside a year — and they did. FERRO STUDIO is the door I built because I refuse to be the fifth. I'm telling you so you don't have to be the sixth."

## THE 3 ORIGIN VARIANTS

### Variant A — Cinderella

**Shape:** Cinderella (Deep low → series of gains → catastrophic reversal → transcendent end)
**Emotional register:** Vindicated, restored, sharp
**Best for:** about-page primary, LinkedIn pinned, the "official" version

> "I started at a 14-person shop in Queens making logos for restaurants that paid in trade. I made it to Wieden+Kennedy by 29 and design director by 35, which was supposed to be the whole story. The reversal arrived in late 2024 when the third brand system I'd built that year was killed in committee by a CFO who'd been on the project for four weeks. FERRO STUDIO is the studio I should have started at 35 and didn't. The roster is four. The work is the work the agency couldn't make room for."

### Variant B — Man-in-Hole

**Shape:** Man-in-Hole (Bearable → fall → recovery → higher than before)
**Emotional register:** Earned, level, recovered
**Best for:** editorial pitch, op-ed lead, founder-pitch email

> "By 39 I had the title, the team of nine, and the salary that explained itself at dinner parties. The fall was a single quarter in 2024 when I shipped two campaigns I knew were beneath the brand and a third that was killed before launch — three for three on work I couldn't defend. The recovery was deciding the floor was the firm and not the work. FERRO STUDIO is the post-recovery position: smaller room, larger work, four clients, $300K minimum, no exceptions."

### Variant C — Kafka

**Shape:** Kafka (Normal → catastrophe → no recovery; the world is permanently altered)
**Emotional register:** Cold, definitional, irreversible
**Best for:** community channel, DM template, deep-fan content, the "real" version

> "The agency I joined in 2013 doesn't exist anymore. The same building, the same name on the door, the same logo — and a different machine inside, retooled for outputs the original room would not have signed. I am not going back, because the place I would go back to was decommissioned in stages no one announced. FERRO STUDIO is not a pivot. It is the acknowledgment that the door behind me closed three years ago and I'm the last one telling you."

| Hook #1 | X — pinned tweet | Day 1 | Highest threshold density. Names W+K and FERRO STUDIO in one line — does the introduction work for any first-time reader. |
| Mythology #1 (Insider Defector) | About page intro | Day 1 | Defector status is the strongest authority claim and the about page is where new buyers land. |
| Origin Variant A (Cinderella) | LinkedIn pinned post | Day 1 | Most universal shape; LinkedIn audience reads it as a credentialing arc. |
| Hook #2 | LinkedIn launch post body | Day 2 | The $450K / $300K hook does the work of making the bet legible to LinkedIn buyers. |
| Mythology #4 (Reluctant Prophet) | Podcast guest intro template | Day 3 | Reluctance + the partner-track detail give a host something to ask about. |
| Hook #4 | Cold-outreach email subject | Day 5 | Lowercase fragment + regret claim outperforms studio-launch announcements in inbox. |
| Mythology #3 (Pattern Spotter) | Pinned tweet (rotation) | Day 7 | Replaces Hook #1 once the launch news is stale; keeps the pinned slot doing thesis work. |
| Origin Variant C (Kafka) | Newsletter for owned audience only | Day 10 | Too cold for the front door. Right register for people who already follow. |
| Mythology #5 (Witness) | Speaking-pitch deck, slide 1 | Day 14+ | Witness frame + W+K specificity is the credential most conferences will book on. |
| Hook #6 | X — anti-audience filter post | Day 21 | Filtration tweet; ships after the launch wave to harden the brief. |
| Origin Variant B (Man-in-Hole) | Op-ed pitch / editorial outreach | Day 21+ | Most polarizing of the three; editorial editors want the recovery arc named. |

# REVERSE COWBOY — Tactical Frameworks

Reference document for the operational playbooks used by the `reverse-cowboy` skill.

## 1. Vivid-Cast Construction Guide

The vivid-cast is not description. It is **neurological programming**. It hijacks the Reticular Activating System, triggers dopaminergic anticipation, and creates the neuroplastic pathways for inevitability.

### The SENSES Checklist
Every vivid-cast must contain at least 5 sensory dimensions:

| Sense | What to Include | Example |
|-------|----------------|---------|
| **Sight** | Specific visual details, colors, screen content, facial expressions | "The Stripe dashboard is a wall of green +$49.00 notifications. Your co-founder's eyes are wide, phone in hand." |
| **Sound** | Notifications, voices, ambient noise, music, internal dialogue | "Slack is pinging with the #growth-alerts waterfall. Someone is laughing in the background. Your inner voice says 'holy shit this is real.'" |
| **Touch/Temperature** | Physical sensations, textures, temperature, pressure | "The coffee cup is warm in your hand. There is cold sweat on your neck. The chair is hard because you haven't moved in 3 hours." |
| **Smell/Taste** | Environmental scents, taste in mouth, food/drink | "The room smells like burnt coffee and adrenaline. Your mouth is dry from not speaking for 20 minutes." |
| **Physiology** | Heart rate, breathing, muscle tension, posture, facial expression | "Your heart rate is 110. You are breathing shallowly. Your face hurts from smiling. Your shoulders are tight with disbelief." |

### The NUMBERS Rule
At least 3 specific numerical metrics must be embedded:
- Not "lots of users" — "847 new signups in the last 4 hours"
- Not "viral tweet" — "12,847 likes, 3,200 retweets, 847 quote tweets saying 'how did I not know about this'"
- Not "making money" — "$14,847 in MRR, churn at 2.1%, CAC at $4.20"

Numbers create **cognitive specificity**. The RAS filters for specificity. Vague numbers are invisible to the RAS.

### The SOCIAL PROOF Layer
At least 2 external validators must be present:
- What specific person said what specific thing
- What specific publication/post/review contains what specific praise
- What specific metric is visible on what specific platform

Social proof creates **emotional contagion**. The brain uses others' reactions to calibrate its own reality assessment.

### The EMOTIONAL ARCHITECTURE
The vivid-cast must contain a **progression of emotions**, not a single static feeling:
1. **The Setup (Neutral/Anticipatory):** "You just sat down. The room is quiet."
2. **The Trigger (Surprise/Disbelief):** "Then you refresh the dashboard."
3. **The Peak (Euphoria/Terror/Disbelief):** "The numbers don't make sense. Your hands are shaking."
4. **The Resolution (Integration/Determination):** "You take a breath. You message the team: 'we need to scale tonight.'"

Emotional progression creates **narrative memory encoding**. A static emotion is forgettable. A progression is unforgettable.

### Vivid-Cast Grading Rubric
| Score | Label | Criteria |
|-------|-------|----------|
| 90-100 | A+ | All 5 senses, 3+ numbers, 2+ social validators, emotional progression, ≥200 words, creates physical sensation in reader |
| 80-89 | A | 4-5 senses, 2-3 numbers, 1-2 social validators, emotional arc present, ≥150 words |
| 70-79 | B | 3-4 senses, 1-2 numbers, 1 social validator, emotional arc weak, ≥100 words |
| 60-69 | C | 2-3 senses, vague numbers, no social validators, static emotion, <100 words |
| <60 | D/F | <2 senses, no numbers, no social proof, flat, <50 words. Rewrite completely. |

## 2. Causal Chain Density Calculator

### The Probability Compound
Each step in the causal chain has a **Step Success Probability (SSP)** — the likelihood that this specific step will be executed successfully. The compound probability of the entire chain is the product of all SSPs.

```
Compound Probability = SSP₁ × SSP₂ × SSP₃ × ... × SSPₙ
```

**Rule:** Compound probability must be ≥50%. If it is <50%, the chain is too fragile for execution.

### SSP Optimization
| SSP | Classification | Action |
|-----|---------------|--------|
| <60% | Fragile | Break into 2-3 sub-steps, add redundancy, or add validation gates |
| 60-75% | Risky | Add a backup plan or pre-validation experiment |
| 75-85% | Acceptable | Add a dopamine milestone to sustain motivation |
| 85-95% | Strong | Monitor for execution. Minimal risk. |
| >95% | Certain | This is a habit, not a step. Merge with adjacent steps. |

### Step Breakdown Protocol
If a step has SSP <60%, break it down using the **SIMPLE breakdown**:
- **S**pecific: What exactly is the action?
- **I**nput: What resource is needed?
- **M**etric: How will success be measured?
- **P**erson: Who is responsible?
- **L**ocation: Where does it happen?
- **E**ndpoint: What is the exact deliverable?

Each sub-step should be executable in <4 hours with no dependencies.

### Causal Density Score
How many steps are causally connected (each step's output is the next step's input)?
- **Low density (<50%):** Many parallel paths, few causal links. Fragile.
- **Medium density (50-75%):** Most steps are causally linked. Stable.
- **High density (75-90%):** Tight causal chain. Each step makes the next inevitable.
- **Extreme density (>90%):** The outcome is mathematically inevitable barring external catastrophe.

**Target:** High density for critical paths. Medium density for non-critical branches.

## 3. Momentum Attribution Framework

### Classification Algorithm
For each step, ask these questions in order:

**Q1: If this step had been skipped, would the outcome still have occurred?**
- No → **Gateway** (40-60% MCP)
- Yes, but at <50% magnitude → **Multiplier** (20-40% MCP)
- Yes, at 70-90% magnitude → **Foundation** (10-20% MCP)
- Yes, at >90% magnitude → **Accelerator** (5-15% MCP)
- Yes, at >95% magnitude → **Noise** (<5% MCP)

**Q2: Did this step unlock subsequent steps, or amplify previous steps?**
- Unlocked subsequent steps → Gateway (if Q1 was "No")
- Amplified previous steps → Multiplier
- Enabled both → Split: 50% Gateway, 50% Multiplier

**Q3: Was the impact of this step visible to the user, or invisible?**
- Visible (viral moment, launch, press) → Higher MCP
- Invisible (infrastructure, preparation, research) → Lower MCP, but still critical

**Q4: Was this step a **point of no return**?**
- Yes (once done, the trajectory was locked in) → +10% MCP bonus
- No (could have been undone or altered) → No bonus

### MCP Revision Triggers
Recalibrate MCP when:
1. A later step is revealed to be the real driver of a previous step's success
2. An assumption is invalidated (e.g., "viral tweet" was actually paid promotion)
3. The premortem identifies a different step as the real failure point
4. Red team analysis argues a step was luck, not causation
5. New information reveals a hidden dependency (Step 7 only worked because of Step 2's trust-building)

### The Momentum Recalibration Formula
```
Recalibrated MCP = Initial MCP × (1 + Premortem Adjustment) × (1 + Red Team Adjustment) × Assumption Validity Score
```

Where:
- **Premortem Adjustment:** -20% to +10% based on whether premortem revealed the step as weaker or stronger than assumed
- **Red Team Adjustment:** -15% to +5% based on skeptic analysis
- **Assumption Validity Score:** 0.5 to 1.0 based on how well the step's core assumptions have been validated

## 4. Adversarial Audit Protocol

### The Premortem Inversion (Phase 1)
Assume the outcome failed completely. Work backward from failure:
1. What was the **last visible sign** that things were going wrong?
2. What was the **first invisible crack** — the moment the causal chain broke beneath the surface?
3. Which step's **assumption** was catastrophically wrong?
4. What **external shock** (competitor, market shift, personal crisis) killed the momentum?
5. If you could save only **one step** from the entire chain, which one would have prevented total collapse?

### The Red Team Pass (Phase 2)
Adopt the persona of a skeptical competitor or venture capitalist who wants to prove this plan is delusional:
1. Which step is **pure luck** dressed up as strategy?
2. Which MCP is **inflated by confirmation bias**?
3. What is the **cheapest, fastest way to falsify** the core assumption of the highest-MCP step?
4. What **alternative explanation** could produce the same outcome with a completely different causal chain?
5. If this were a competitor's plan, which step would you **exploit** to destroy their outcome?

### The Assumption Stress Test (Phase 3)
For each step, list the **3 most critical assumptions** and define a validation experiment:

| Assumption | Falsification Criteria | Validation Experiment | Time Box | Cost |
|------------|----------------------|----------------------|----------|------|
| "Users will share the product organically" | <5% organic sharing rate in first 100 users | Launch to 100 beta users, track sharing without prompts | 2 weeks | $0 |
| "The content will resonate with target audience" | <10% engagement rate on test posts | Post 5 test pieces to target community, measure engagement | 1 week | $0 |

**Rule:** Any assumption without a validation experiment is a **faith-based step** and must have its MCP capped at 50% until validated.

### The Momentum Recalibration (Phase 4)
After Phases 1-3, recalibrate every MCP:
- Gateway steps that fail premortem → demote to Multiplier or Foundation
- Steps flagged by red team as "luck" → cap at Accelerator max (15%)
- Steps with unvalidated assumptions → cap at 50%
- Steps that survive all three audits with no revision → promote by 5-10%

## 5. Neural Rehearsal Scripts

### The Richardson Protocol
Based on Richardson's 1967 basketball study (visualization improved performance 23%, nearly matching physical practice at 24%):

Every critical step must have a **3-sentence neural rehearsal script**:
1. **Visual:** "See yourself [specific action in specific environment]."
2. **Somatic:** "Feel [specific physical sensation — temperature, pressure, muscle activation]."
3. **Emotional:** "The result is [specific outcome]. You feel [specific emotion — not 'happy,' but 'the warmth of pride spreading from your chest to your shoulders']."

### The Mirror Neuron Activation
Mental rehearsal works because the brain's **mirror neuron system** fires both when performing an action AND when vividly imagining it. The neural rehearsal script must be vivid enough to trigger mirror neuron activation.

**Quality Check:** If the user cannot generate a physical response (increased heart rate, slight muscle tension, facial expression change) while reading the script, it is not vivid enough. Add sensory specificity.

### Daily Rehearsal Cadence
- **Morning (upon waking):** Rehearse the next 3 steps on the critical path. 2 minutes each.
- **Pre-action (5 minutes before):** Rehearse the specific step about to be executed. 1 minute.
- **Evening (before sleep):** Rehearse the vivid-cast in its entirety. 3 minutes.

**Neuroplasticity Rule:** Repetition + emotional engagement = neural pathway strengthening. The rehearsal must be emotionally loaded, not mechanically repeated.

## 6. Dopamine Milestone Engineering

### The Dopamine Reset Window
Dopamine circuits fatigue after 7-10 days without a reward signal. The milestone map must have **no gaps >7 days** without a visible, measurable win.

### Milestone Types
| Type | Frequency | Example | Dopamine Trigger |
|------|-----------|---------|-----------------|
| **Micro-win** | Daily | Send 5 DMs, write 1 paragraph, fix 1 bug | Completion checkmark, small metric tick |
| **Mini-win** | Every 2-3 days | First reply from target user, first share, first bug report | External validation, social signal |
| **Mid-win** | Weekly | 10 new users, 1 content piece published, 1 feature shipped | Visible progress metric, community reaction |
| **Major milestone** | Every 2-4 weeks | 100 users, viral moment, first revenue, press mention | Big number, social proof, public validation |
| **Gateway milestone** | At gateway steps only | Product launch, content drop, partnership signed | Irreversible commitment, point of no return |

### The Variable Ratio Principle
Predictable rewards create satisfaction. **Unpredictable** rewards create addiction. Inject 1-2 "surprise milestones" per month — unexpected wins that were not on the map but release dopamine when they occur.

### Dopamine Dead Zone Detection
Scan the milestone map for any 10+ day period with no visible validation signal. If found:
1. Insert a micro-win or mini-win in the dead zone
2. Or redesign the preceding step to produce an earlier validation signal
3. Or split a long step into two shorter steps with an intermediate milestone

## 7. Gravity Score Calculation

The Gravity Score measures the **inevitability** of the outcome. It is a weighted average of 5 dimensions:

| Dimension | Weight | Score (0-100) | Measurement |
|-----------|--------|---------------|-------------|
| **Gravitational Mass** | 1.2x | How much "pull" the vivid-cast creates. Measured by vivid-cast grade (A+ = 95, A = 85, B = 75, etc.) | Vivid-cast rubric |
| **RAS Engagement** | 1.0x | How specifically the vivid-cast programs the Reticular Activating System. Measured by specificity score (5 senses + 3 numbers + 2 social validators = 100) | SENSES checklist |
| **Neural Pathway Strength** | 1.0x | How rehearsed and automatic the critical path feels. Measured by neural rehearsal quality × repetition cadence | Neural rehearsal rubric |
| **Dopamine Loop Integrity** | 1.2x | Whether motivation will sustain. Measured by absence of dead zones + variable ratio injection + milestone frequency | Milestone map audit |
| **Causal Density** | 1.5x | Whether the chain is dense enough to make alternatives improbable. Measured by compound probability × causal connection percentage | Causal chain calculator |

**Overall Gravity Score =**
```
(Gravitational Mass×1.2 + RAS Engagement×1.0 + Neural Pathway Strength×1.0 + Dopamine Loop Integrity×1.2 + Causal Density×1.5) / 5.9
```

| Score | Interpretation | Action |
|-------|---------------|--------|
| 90-100 | **Inevitability** | The outcome is mathematically and neurologically programmed. Execute. |
| 80-89 | **Probable** | Strong chain. Minor refinements recommended. Execute with monitoring. |
| 70-79 | **Possible** | Fragile chain. Requires adversarial audit and step breakdown before execution. |
| 60-69 | **Uncertain** | Major revisions needed. Do not execute. Return to vivid-cast and causal chain. |
| <60 | **Delusion** | The plan is wishful thinking. Rewrite from scratch with higher specificity. |

## 9. Execution Discipline

### The 7-Day Rule
No blueprint is valid for >7 days without execution. After 7 days of inaction, the neural pathways decay, the vivid-cast fades, and the dopamine loop breaks. If the user has not executed Day 1 within 7 days of blueprint creation, the blueprint must be **refreshed** — vivid-cast rewritten, causal chain recalibrated, MCPs reassessed.

### The Daily Commitment Protocol
Each morning, the user must:
1. Read the vivid-cast aloud (2 minutes)
2. Rehearse the next critical step (2 minutes)
3. Execute the day's specific action (defined in the blueprint)
4. Log the validation signal (30 seconds)
5. Celebrate the dopamine trigger (15 seconds — this is not optional; it closes the dopamine loop)

### The Weekly Audit
Every 7 days, the user must run a mini adversarial audit:
1. Which steps were executed? Which were skipped?
2. Did the validation signals match expectations?
3. Which assumptions were validated? Which were falsified?
4. Do MCPs need recalibration based on new data?
5. Is the compound probability still ≥50%?

### The Kill Criteria
Define in advance the signals that indicate the blueprint should be abandoned:
- **Time kill:** No meaningful validation signal after 21 days
- **Assumption kill:** Core assumption falsified by experiment
- **Market kill:** External shift makes the outcome impossible (regulatory change, competitor lock-in, technology obsolescence)
- **Motivation kill:** Gravity Score drops below 60 for 2 consecutive weeks

Kill criteria are not failure. They are **intelligent exit points** that preserve energy for a better blueprint.

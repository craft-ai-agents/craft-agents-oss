# TONGUE-ME — Rubric & Scoring Guide

## Scoring Scale

| Score | Label | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| 90-100 | A | Sovereign. The artifact filters its audience. The reader auditions to enter. |
| 80-89  | B | Strong prestige register. Minor pursuing-voice contamination. |
| 70-79  | C | Mid-register. Some sovereign moves, undermined by performance warmth. |
| 60-69  | D | Pursuing voice with prestige decoration. The brand still wants the reader's permission. |
| 50-59  | F | Fully pursuing. Universal welcome. Hedge-saturated. The brand needs the reader. |
| 0-49   | F- | Begging voice. The artifact reads as a request to be considered. |

## Pillar 1: Stance (Weight 1.2x)

**Question:** Are statements made as weather, or hedged as opinions?

| Score | Hedge Inventory | Critical-Position Hedges | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 90+ | 0 hedges across artifact | 0 | Declarative absolutes throughout |
| 80-89 | 1-3 hedges, none critical | 0 | Mostly declarative |
| 70-79 | 4-6 hedges, none critical | 0 | Hedge contamination visible but contained |
| 60-69 | Any number, 1+ in critical position | 1 | Hero/headline hedged — score capped |
| 50-59 | Hedge-saturated | 2+ | Voice has no spine |
| <50 | Every claim wrapped | 3+ | The brand does not believe its own copy |

**Critical positions:**
- Hero / headline (first H1 or equivalent)
- Subhead / dek (first H2 or supporting line)
- Primary CTA copy (the button text and the line above it)
- Climax (the final ask before footer)

A hedge in any of these = score capped at 60 regardless of body cleanliness.

## Pillar 2: Distance (Weight 1.2x)

**Question:** Is the emotional temperature calibrated, or does the voice perform warmth out of need?

| Score | Performance-Warmth Markers | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 90+ | 0-1 across artifact | Calibrated coldness; warmth shown sparingly |
| 80-89 | 2-3, none in hero | Mostly cold; one or two performance moments |
| 70-79 | 4-5, ≤1 in hero | Performance creeping in |
| 60-69 | 6-8, multiple in hero | Performance warmth dominates |
| 50-59 | 9+, saturated | "So excited!" voice |
| <50 | Saturated + emoji enthusiasm + multiple ! | Brand is begging for permission to exist |

**Performance-warmth markers:**
- "So excited," "Excited to," "I'm thrilled"
- "Can't wait," "Looking forward to"
- "Love to," "Would love"
- "Honored," "Privileged," "Grateful"
- Multiple exclamation points
- Enthusiasm emoji (🎉 ✨ 💫 🙏 ❤️) outside personal-letter contexts

**Exception:** personal letters, condolences, and contexts where performance warmth is content-appropriate are exempt. Brand voice is never exempt.

## Pillar 3: Polarity (Weight 1.0x)

**Question:** Does the copy create a binary love/hate response, or live in mass-appeal middle?

| Score | Binary Response Test | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 90+ | A specific demographic will reject this. The 30% who remain become evangelists. | Polarized |
| 80-89 | Mild friction. Some will disagree. | Strong stance, soft delivery |
| 70-79 | Differentiated but uncontroversial. | Mid |
| 60-69 | Universal appeal attempted. | Mass-market |
| 50-59 | "Whether you're a beginner or expert..." | Pre-resigned to invisibility |
| <50 | "For everyone who..." | Total flatness |

**The Binary Response Test:** Read the artifact. Could 30%+ of the audience reject it on first read? If no, polarity is absent.

## Pillar 4: Filtration (Weight 1.4x)

**Question:** Does the copy filter the audience or pursue it?

| Score | Disqualifier Density | Status Posture | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 90+ | 2+ disqualifiers in first 100 words | Reader auditions | Active filtration |
| 80-89 | 1 disqualifier in first 100 words | Reader vets self | Filtration present |
| 70-79 | Disqualifiers in body, not hero | Reader is asked to qualify late | Soft filtration |
| 60-69 | Implied criteria only | Reader is gently informed | Implied filtration |
| 50-59 | No filtration | Reader is welcomed by default | Universal welcome |
| <50 | Active welcome of all | Brand pursues all | Pursuing voice |

**Disqualifier markers:**
- "We accept ___ candidates per ___"
- "If ___, this isn't for you"
- "Apply if you meet…"
- "We work with ___ only"
- "Not for ___"
- "Wrong fit if ___"
- "[Threshold] required"

This is the highest-weighted pillar (1.4x) because filtration is the most predictive signal of sovereign voice. A brand can fail every other pillar and still feel pursued if the filtration is sharp.

## Pillar 5: Mystique (Weight 1.1x)

**Question:** Does the copy say less than the reader wants?

| Score | Withholding Density | Anti-Mystique Markers | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 90+ | Mechanism, inventory, roster all withheld | 0 anti-mystique | Strategic restraint |
| 80-89 | At least 2 of 3 withheld | 0-1 | Mostly restrained |
| 70-79 | 1 of 3 withheld | 2-3 | Even balance |
| 60-69 | All explained | 4-5 | Over-explained |
| 50-59 | All explained, anti-mystique saturated | 6+ | Total transparency |
| <50 | Every benefit listed, every objection pre-handled | 8+ | Reader has no reason to lean in |

**Anti-mystique markers:**
- "What this means is…"
- "In other words…"
- "Let me explain…"
- "To put it simply…"
- "The way this works is…"
- Defining audience-known terms
- Pre-handling objections
- Bullet-listed benefits in mass-market formats

## Pillar 6: Cadence (Weight 1.1x)

**Question:** Does the prose have percussion?

| Score | Sentence-Length Variance | Single-Word Sentences | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 90+ | High variance (StdDev > 7) | 1-3 across artifact | Asymmetric rhythm |
| 80-89 | Moderate variance (StdDev 5-7) | 0-1 | Some rhythmic variation |
| 70-79 | Low variance (StdDev 3-5) | 0 | Mostly uniform |
| 60-69 | Uniform sequences (StdDev <3) | 0 | Flat exposition |
| 50-59 | Total uniformity | 0 | AI-prose register |
| <50 | All sentences within ±2 words | 0 | Reader's ear has nothing to grab |

**The Sentence-Length Sequence Test:**
- Extract every sentence in the artifact
- Count words per sentence
- Calculate standard deviation across the sequence
- High variance = percussive
- Low variance = flat

**Reference sequences:**
- Sovereign (StdDev 8.4): `4, 12, 18, 3, 8, 22, 5, 1, 14, 9, 4, 16`
- Mid (StdDev 4.1): `9, 14, 11, 12, 8, 13, 10, 15, 9, 12`
- Flat (StdDev 1.7): `12, 13, 12, 14, 13, 12, 13, 14, 12`

## Overall Score Calculation

```
weighted_sum = (Stance × 1.2) + (Distance × 1.2) + (Polarity × 1.0)
             + (Filtration × 1.4) + (Mystique × 1.1) + (Cadence × 1.1)
total_weights = 7.0
overall_score = weighted_sum / total_weights
```

Filtration is weighted highest (1.4x) because it is the most predictive single signal of sovereign voice. Stance and Distance are tied second (1.2x) because they catch the most common failure modes (hedging, performance warmth). Polarity is base-weighted because it can be earned through other pillars. Mystique and Cadence are slightly above base because they are the texture pillars that separate B+ from A.

## Status Inversion Engine — Bonus Score

After the pillar score is calculated, apply the SIE bonus/penalty:

| SIE Moves Detected (in first 100 words) | Adjustment |
|---|---|
| 3/3 (Refusal + Filter + Application) | +5 |
| 2/3 | +0 (neutral) |
| 1/3 | -3 |
| 0/3 | -8 (caps overall score at 75) |

The reasoning: a brand can score well on pillars but fail to claim sovereignty if the three architectural moves are missing. Conversely, a brand with all three moves earns the prestige bonus even if line-level execution is mid.

## Artifact-Type Adjustments

### Marketing copy (web, email, ads, decks)
- All pillars apply at full weight
- SIE bonus active
- This is the canonical use case

### Personal-letter copy (1:1 emails, DMs, condolences)
- Distance pillar **inverted** — performance warmth is appropriate
- Filtration pillar **disabled** — disqualification is wrong context
- Use `--register=warm` flag

### Sales pages (high-conversion direct response)
- Mystique pillar **lowered weight to 0.7x** — direct response benefits from explicit benefit-listing
- Filtration pillar **stays at 1.4x** — high-converting sales pages still filter

### Editorial / opinion content
- All pillars apply
- Polarity weighted +0.3 (becomes 1.3x) — editorial requires sharper polarity
- Use `--register=editorial` flag

### Product UI copy (buttons, errors, microcopy)
- Distance and Mystique only
- Stance pillar **lowered weight to 0.5x** — UI copy can be functional
- Use `--context=ui` flag
